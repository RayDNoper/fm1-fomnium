FoMni-1 chord sets

The chords on the black keys, eight sets of them. Change them here and the FM-1 plays them at once; PRESETS on the FM-1 picks the set.

Not connected. Plug the FM-1 in by USB (Chrome or Edge), or open the emulator in another tab and switch it on.

A file holds all eight sets as chord names, so it can also be written by hand. Loading one replaces every set on the FM-1. The FM-1 saves by itself a few seconds after a change, once it is quiet.
